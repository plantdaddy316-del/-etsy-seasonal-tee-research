# Seasonal T-Shirt Test Concepts
**Prepared:** October 12, 2026  
**Use:** Low-risk Etsy listing tests. The three designs are original, unbranded concepts; conduct a final trademark/search clearance before publishing.

## 1. Porchlight Club — Halloween / late autumn
**Mockup:** `porchlight-club.svg`  
**Design:** Warm, nostalgic black-cat-and-pumpkin graphic on a washed-black tee. Copy: *PORCHLIGHT CLUB — FIND YOUR WAY HOME.*  
**Customer angle:** Cozy Halloween buyers who prefer seasonal atmosphere over costumes or fandom graphics.  
**Proposed Etsy title:** Porchlight Club Halloween Shirt, Cozy Black Cat Pumpkin Tee, Vintage Spooky Season Gift  
**Tags to test:** halloween shirt; black cat tee; pumpkin shirt; cozy halloween; spooky season; fall graphic tee; autumn shirt; cat lover gift; retro halloween; porchlight club; witchy fall style; october outfit; unisex tee  
**Test variable:** Offer washed black, pepper, and burnt orange blanks. Main image should be the mockup, then a close crop of the artwork.

## 2. Thankful for My Quiet Morning — Thanksgiving / autumn
**Mockup:** `thankful-quiet-morning.svg`  
**Design:** Oatmeal tee with coffee, steam, leaves, and a calm typographic message: *THANKFUL FOR MY QUIET MORNING.*  
**Customer angle:** Adult fall/Thanksgiving shoppers seeking a low-key, non-family-reunion slogan.  
**Proposed Etsy title:** Thankful for My Quiet Morning Shirt, Coffee Lover Thanksgiving Tee, Cozy Fall Gift  
**Tags to test:** thankful shirt; thanksgiving tee; coffee lover gift; quiet morning; fall coffee shirt; autumn graphic tee; cozy season tee; gratitude shirt; november outfit; hostess gift; introvert shirt; harvest season; unisex fall tee  
**Test variable:** Compare a literal coffee-first first photo against the typography-first mockup; use oatmeal and sand blanks.

## 3. Wrapped Up, Not Rushed — winter gifting
**Mockup:** `wrapped-up-not-rushed.svg`  
**Design:** Forest green tee with a retro gift box and message: *WRAPPED UP / NOT RUSHED.*  
**Customer angle:** A slower, calmer alternative to generic holiday-party slogans; viable from early November through December.  
**Proposed Etsy title:** Wrapped Up Not Rushed Christmas Shirt, Cozy Holiday Gift Tee, Minimal Winter Graphic  
**Tags to test:** christmas shirt; holiday shirt; cozy christmas; winter graphic tee; gift wrapping shirt; slow holiday; christmas gift; festive tee; december outfit; forest green tee; minimalist holiday; holiday party tee; unisex christmas  
**Test variable:** Test forest green versus vintage cream blanks; include a giftable folded-shirt photo as image two.

## Testing sequence
1. Publish as three separate listings with 2–3 color variants and print-on-demand fulfillment only after clearance.
2. Run each for seven days with the same price, shipping policy, and listing-image count; do not change multiple variables at once.
3. Keep the design with the best combination of listing visits, favorites, and conversion; retire or rework the weakest after the first comparable traffic window.

Source: https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/category/seasonal-tips
- Google’s Frightgeist describes Halloween costume interest as spiking in October. This supports the timing of the Halloween concept, but it does **not** validate demand for any specific shirt phrase. Source: https://frightgeist.withgoogle.com/about

## Could not confirm
- Current Etsy search volume, click-through rate, conversion, and competition for these exact phrases.
- Whether any phrase is already registered or otherwise problematic; perform a trademark check and Etsy search before listing.

**Confidence:** Moderate that the concepts fit recognizable seasonal buying windows; low-to-moderate that any one slogan will convert until a controlled listing test supplies traffic data.
